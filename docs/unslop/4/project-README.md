# Orbel

A palm-sized glass light for your desk that keeps your focus time. Turn the top to set a session; the light fills
the orb and slowly drains as the minutes pass, so you always know how much is left without looking at a screen.

> Orbel is an invented product made for a demo. It is not a real device.

## How it works

1. **Twist to start.** Turn the aluminium cap: each click adds 5 minutes, up to 90. The orb fills with warm light.
2. **Watch it drain.** The light sinks from the top down as time passes. At 5 minutes left it shifts from warm white
   to amber.
3. **Tap to pause, lift to stop.** Tap the glass to pause. Lift Orbel off its base and the session ends.
4. **Break glow.** When a session ends, Orbel breathes slowly in soft blue for your 5 minute break.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 72 mm wide, 88 mm tall, 240 g |
| Body | Frosted borosilicate glass orb, bead-blasted aluminium cap and base |
| Light | 24 warm and cool LEDs, 2700 K to 5000 K, plus amber and blue |
| Battery | 1,800 mAh, about 2 weeks of daily sessions; charges on the base by USB-C |
| Apps | None needed. An optional app shows your weekly focus hours. |

## Look

Frosted glass that glows from inside, brushed aluminium in graphite or silver, a soft shadow on a wooden desk. The
brand colours are warm white `#FFE9C7`, amber `#FFB347` and graphite `#2B2D31`. The name is set in a thin, wide
geometric sans.
