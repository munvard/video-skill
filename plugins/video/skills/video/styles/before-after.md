# Before and after

Family: Screen and UI. Status: draft.

One problem, two worlds.

- **Use it for:** Anything with a visible change.
- **Not for:** A change that cannot be shown honestly.
- **Look:** The two sides share camera and framing, only the subject changes.
- **Motion:** One wipe, fold or match, held long on both sides.
- **Type:** Two or three words in total.
- **Sound:** The mess is loud, the result is quiet.
- **Build with:** Any engine, identical cameras.
- **Hook:** The worst second of the problem.
- **It fails when:** A fake or unfair before.
