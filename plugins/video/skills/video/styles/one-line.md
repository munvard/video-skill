# One continuous line

Family: Minimal motion. Status: draft.

A single pen never leaves the page.

- **Use it for:** Stories, journeys, health, education.
- **Not for:** Detailed interface proof.
- **Look:** Paper color, one ink color, rare fill.
- **Motion:** The line draws, loops and becomes each object. The camera follows the pen.
- **Type:** Handwritten or light serif, a few words.
- **Sound:** Pen on paper, solo piano or strings.
- **Build with:** SVG path length or canvas.
- **Hook:** The line makes something you recognise late.
- **It fails when:** The line breaks, or many lines appear.
