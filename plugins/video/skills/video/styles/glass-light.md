# Glass and light

Family: Cinematic 3D. Status: draft.

Calm, expensive and abstract.

- **Use it for:** AI, audio, finance, a brand film.
- **Not for:** When people must learn a feature.
- **Look:** Thick glass forms, caustics, one color family, soft grain.
- **Motion:** Forms melt and join very slowly with one faster turn.
- **Type:** Thin large words refracted through glass.
- **Sound:** Warm pads, glass tones tuned to the music.
- **Build with:** Blender Cycles, few objects, high samples.
- **Hook:** Light passes through a shape and becomes a word.
- **It fails when:** It becomes a screensaver with no idea.
