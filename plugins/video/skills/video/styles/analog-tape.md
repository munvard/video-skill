# Analog tape

Family: Handmade and graphic. Status: draft.

A found recording.

- **Use it for:** Teasers, mystery, music.
- **Not for:** Clear product steps.
- **Look:** Soft contrast, tracking, real timecode look, small flaws.
- **Motion:** Handheld, sudden zooms, one clean moment.
- **Type:** Camera text, rare.
- **Sound:** Tape hiss, room sound.
- **Build with:** Real footage or 3D with a tape pass.
- **Hook:** Something you should not have filmed.
- **It fails when:** A filter over normal shots.
