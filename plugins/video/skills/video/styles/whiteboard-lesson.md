# Whiteboard lesson

Family: Handmade and graphic. Status: draft.

A good teacher draws it for you.

- **Use it for:** Explainers, education, hard ideas.
- **Not for:** Emotional brand films.
- **Look:** One board, two pens, big drawings.
- **Motion:** The drawing appears with the spoken idea. The camera drifts with the ink.
- **Type:** Written large, erased when done.
- **Sound:** A natural voice, marker, room tone.
- **Build with:** SVG strokes or 3D slate.
- **Hook:** The wrong answer most people believe.
- **It fails when:** Long text, a drawing that repeats the voice.
