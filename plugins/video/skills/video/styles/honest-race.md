# Honest race

Family: Screen and UI. Status: draft.

Two ways, one clock.

- **Use it for:** A real time saving that you measured.
- **Not for:** No measurement, or a tiny gain.
- **Look:** Split frame, same task, same clock, clear finish.
- **Motion:** Real time, or a speed-up shown with the clock.
- **Type:** The clock and two plain names.
- **Sound:** A ticking pulse that stops at the finish.
- **Build with:** Two real recordings in sync.
- **Hook:** The fast side is nearly done.
- **It fails when:** Edited clocks, a weak rival chosen on purpose.
