# Perfect loop

Family: Social speed. Status: draft.

You watch it again before you notice.

- **Use it for:** Short posts, satisfying products.
- **Not for:** A story with an ending.
- **Look:** One space, matched first and last frame.
- **Motion:** One continuous action that feeds itself.
- **Type:** At most one line, readable at any time.
- **Sound:** Audio also loops.
- **Build with:** 3D or canvas, seam measured.
- **Hook:** The action is already in the middle.
- **It fails when:** A jump in light, sound or speed at the seam.
