# Native low-fi

Family: Story and comedy. Status: draft.

A friend made it on a phone.

- **Use it for:** Daily social posts, tests of an idea.
- **Not for:** A premium launch.
- **Look:** Platform-native framing, real phone image, default captions.
- **Motion:** Simple cuts, no effects.
- **Type:** Plain caption text.
- **Sound:** Phone voice, room.
- **Build with:** Phone or a plain capture.
- **Hook:** A true strange fact, said fast.
- **It fails when:** Pretending to be a customer.
