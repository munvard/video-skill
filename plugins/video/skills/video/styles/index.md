# Style index

Read this file, then open only the one or two style files you chose.

Status: **proven** (a film made by this skill in this style passed every gate and is shown in the README with its prompt), **studied** (measured from real work with `scripts/study.py`, the numbers saved next to the card), **draft** (designed from craft knowledge, not tested yet). Cards marked with a star have full depth: world, camera grammar, three shapes, signature moves, build notes.

## For launch and product films, start here
One take with a thread, Floating layers, Screen cinema, The cursor is the hero, Glass and light, Macro material, Exploded view, Kinetic type, Swiss grid, Shape morph, Data in motion, Beat cut. Mix them: a launch film often uses one spine style and two or three others for its worlds.
