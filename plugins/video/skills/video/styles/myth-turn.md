# Myth and turn

Family: Story and comedy. Status: draft.

An argument with a turn in the middle.

- **Use it for:** Opinion, education, tools people use badly.
- **Not for:** No real myth.
- **Look:** A new picture every 2 to 3 seconds that follows the argument.
- **Motion:** Push into a screen, dive into an object, return to the first place.
- **Type:** Word by word with the voice, one color for the word that matters.
- **Sound:** A nonstop natural voice, music with a real build.
- **Build with:** Mixed 3D and 2D.
- **Hook:** The question everyone asks, then "wrong question".
- **It fails when:** A weak straw man, numbered steps, fake scarcity.
