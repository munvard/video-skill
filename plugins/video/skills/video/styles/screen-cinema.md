# Screen cinema

Family: Screen and UI. Status: draft.

A real take filmed like a movie.

- **Use it for:** Proof that a feature works.
- **Not for:** A feature that is ugly or slow on screen.
- **Look:** A clean real recording on a lit 3D display, with depth and reflections.
- **Motion:** The camera moves to where the cursor goes and waits there.
- **Type:** One line of context, never over the action.
- **Sound:** Real clicks, quiet bed.
- **Build with:** A scripted recording as a texture in 3D.
- **Hook:** The result is already on screen.
- **It fails when:** It looks like a plain screen recording.
