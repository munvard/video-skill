# Scrapbook

Family: Handmade and graphic. Status: draft.

A private book being filled.

- **Use it for:** Founder stories, community, year reviews.
- **Not for:** Corporate news.
- **Look:** Photos, tape, stickers, pencil notes, warm paper.
- **Motion:** Page turns and quick placements.
- **Type:** Handwriting that can be read.
- **Sound:** Camera clicks, pages, soft guitar.
- **Build with:** 2D layers, your own photos only.
- **Hook:** One photo with a question written on it.
- **It fails when:** Clutter, stock photos.
