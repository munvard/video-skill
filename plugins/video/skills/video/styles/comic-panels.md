# Comic panels

Family: Handmade and graphic. Status: draft.

A page that comes alive.

- **Use it for:** Problem stories, humor, security.
- **Not for:** Quiet luxury.
- **Look:** Ink lines, flat color, strong black, panel borders.
- **Motion:** The camera goes through panels, sound words react.
- **Type:** Short speech balloons.
- **Sound:** Ink hits, brass or drums.
- **Build with:** 2D layers, original art.
- **Hook:** A panel where everything goes wrong.
- **It fails when:** Too many balloons.
