# Beat cut

Family: Social speed. Status: proven.

Loud, fast, in rhythm.

- **Use it for:** Young audiences, sports, games, teasers.
- **Not for:** Trust topics, older viewers.
- **Look:** Strong contrast, one look, a new picture on each beat.
- **Motion:** Hits on beats, real motion blur, one sudden stop.
- **Type:** One word a beat, huge.
- **Sound:** The track leads, a real drop.
- **Build with:** Motion kit or footage.
- **Hook:** The best shot first.
- **It fails when:** Same energy for the whole film, flash effects.
