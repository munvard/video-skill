# Isometric city

Family: Cinematic 3D. Status: draft.

A living map from above.

- **Use it for:** Logistics, platforms, marketplaces, systems.
- **Not for:** Personal or emotional stories.
- **Look:** Isometric camera, clean blocks, long soft shadows, day to night.
- **Motion:** The world builds itself tile by tile. Cars and people never stop.
- **Type:** Short tags on roofs, large.
- **Sound:** Light ticks for each tile, a steady pulse.
- **Build with:** Blender orthographic camera or canvas.
- **Hook:** One tile drops and a city grows from it.
- **It fails when:** Too many tiny parts, no hero to follow.
