# Floating layers

Family: Screen and UI. Status: draft.

The interface opens in space.

- **Use it for:** Design tools, dashboards, systems.
- **Not for:** A single simple screen.
- **Look:** Glass or paper layers at real depth, bright world, soft shadows.
- **Motion:** Layers separate, one comes forward, the rest lose focus.
- **Type:** Names only for the layer in focus.
- **Sound:** Soft air and clicks by depth.
- **Build with:** WebGL or Blender planes with thickness.
- **Hook:** A flat screen turns sideways and has depth.
- **It fails when:** Swinging panels, small text, no focus.
