# Clay stop motion

Family: Cinematic 3D. Status: draft.

Warm, handmade and a bit imperfect.

- **Use it for:** Friendly apps, food, kids, wellness, a mascot.
- **Not for:** Sharp technical proof.
- **Look:** Fingerprints in clay, felt and card sets, soft window light.
- **Motion:** Animate on twos (12 poses a second). Nothing moves with a perfect curve.
- **Type:** Words made of clay or cut card.
- **Sound:** Close tactile sounds, ukulele or soft piano.
- **Build with:** Blender with displacement and stepped keys, or real photos.
- **Hook:** A lump of clay becomes the product.
- **It fails when:** Motion is smooth 60 fps, surfaces look like plain plastic.
