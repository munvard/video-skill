# Riso print

Family: Handmade and graphic. Status: draft.

Two inks slightly out of line.

- **Use it for:** Music, events, indie products.
- **Not for:** Luxury or medical.
- **Look:** Two or three spot inks, halftone, paper grain, misregistration.
- **Motion:** Stepped layers and frame by frame loops.
- **Type:** Bold poster type.
- **Sound:** Drum machine, print roller.
- **Build with:** Canvas with halftone and multiply.
- **Hook:** One ink becomes a full picture when the second arrives.
- **It fails when:** Texture over an ordinary layout.
