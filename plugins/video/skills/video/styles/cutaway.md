# Cutaway

Family: Data and explanation. Status: draft.

The wall opens and you see how it works.

- **Use it for:** Security, engineering, biology, infrastructure.
- **Not for:** Secrets or parts you cannot verify.
- **Look:** A section through an object, colored by function.
- **Motion:** One particle travels the whole path, the camera follows.
- **Type:** A word per stage, large.
- **Sound:** A sound for each stage.
- **Build with:** Blender booleans or layered 2D.
- **Hook:** A familiar thing is cut in half.
- **It fails when:** A path nobody can follow.
