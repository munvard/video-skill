# Data in motion

Family: Data and explanation. Status: draft.

One number becomes something you can see.

- **Use it for:** Research, reports, results you can cite.
- **Not for:** Data without a source.
- **Look:** One chart at a time, physical scale, quiet color.
- **Motion:** Axis first, then the surprise, then a hold.
- **Type:** Units, source, one sentence.
- **Sound:** Ticks by value, a stop at the surprise.
- **Build with:** SVG, canvas or 3D.
- **Hook:** The number before its meaning.
- **It fails when:** A cut axis, many charts.
