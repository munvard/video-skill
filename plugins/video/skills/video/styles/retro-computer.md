# Retro computer

Family: Handmade and graphic. Status: draft.

An invented old computer runs your idea.

- **Use it for:** Developer products, launches with humor.
- **Not for:** Copying a real company's system.
- **Look:** Own window design, thick pixels, curved glass, scan lines.
- **Motion:** Windows open on beats, slow draws used as tension.
- **Type:** Bitmap type, few words.
- **Sound:** Drive noise, beeps, hum.
- **Build with:** Canvas on a 3D tube.
- **Hook:** The old machine says something too modern.
- **It fails when:** Looks like a real brand's product.
