# Soft gradient

Family: Minimal motion. Status: draft.

Breathing color and one sentence.

- **Use it for:** Meditation, sleep, journaling, a quiet launch.
- **Not for:** Anything urgent.
- **Look:** Slow color fields with grain, one floating object.
- **Motion:** 10 to 20 second drifts, nothing faster than a breath.
- **Type:** Large light words, long holds.
- **Sound:** Warm pads, room tone, long tails.
- **Build with:** Shader or Blender volume.
- **Hook:** A color you want to stay in, with one honest line.
- **It fails when:** No idea under the color, banding, boring middle.
