# Blueprint

Family: Minimal motion. Status: draft.

The drawing becomes the real thing.

- **Use it for:** Hardware, architecture, developer tools.
- **Not for:** Beauty or lifestyle.
- **Look:** Deep blue or white plan, thin dimension lines, then full material.
- **Motion:** Lines draw, the camera tilts, the plan rises into 3D.
- **Type:** Small technical captions, one big claim.
- **Sound:** Pencil, plotter, one warm hit at the material change.
- **Build with:** SVG into Blender with matched cameras.
- **Hook:** A plan draws itself at speed.
- **It fails when:** Fake measurements, unreadable small text.
