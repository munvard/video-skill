# Map journey

Family: Data and explanation. Status: draft.

One dot crosses the world.

- **Use it for:** Travel, shipping, networks, history.
- **Not for:** A story with no place.
- **Look:** A simple map that becomes real terrain.
- **Motion:** Fly, land, leave. One route color.
- **Type:** Place names only.
- **Sound:** Wind, place sounds, pulse.
- **Build with:** Open map data with credit, Blender terrain.
- **Hook:** A dot in the wrong place.
- **It fails when:** Spinning globe with no story.
