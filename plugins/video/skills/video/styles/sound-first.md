# Sound first

Family: Live footage. Status: draft.

You hear it before you understand it.

- **Use it for:** Food, tools, keyboards, craft.
- **Not for:** Voice-led explanation.
- **Look:** Macro shots ordered by sound.
- **Motion:** Cuts follow the sounds, which build a beat.
- **Type:** None until the end.
- **Sound:** The edit is the music.
- **Build with:** Real sounds, or careful foley.
- **Hook:** A single perfect sound.
- **It fails when:** Music laid over it.
