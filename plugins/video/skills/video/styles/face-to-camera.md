# Face to camera

Family: Live footage. Status: draft.

One person says one true thing.

- **Use it for:** Founders, opinions, trust.
- **Not for:** Nothing specific to say.
- **Look:** A window light, eye contact, a simple place.
- **Motion:** Stillness, one cut per idea, graphics only to prove.
- **Type:** Captions by phrase, the key word in color.
- **Sound:** Clean close voice.
- **Build with:** Phone or camera, a proof insert.
- **Hook:** The sentence people will argue with.
- **It fails when:** A long hello, a read script.
