# Pixel art

Family: Handmade and graphic. Status: draft.

A small original world in pixels.

- **Use it for:** Indie tools, nostalgia, communities.
- **Not for:** A copy of an existing game.
- **Look:** A fixed low resolution, a strict palette, your own characters.
- **Motion:** Frame by frame, parallax, camera snaps to pixels.
- **Type:** A readable bitmap font at large scale.
- **Sound:** Original chip sounds.
- **Build with:** Canvas at integer scale.
- **Hook:** A tiny hero in trouble.
- **It fails when:** Mixed pixel sizes, borrowed characters.
