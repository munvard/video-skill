# Chat story

Family: Social speed. Status: draft.

A conversation with a turn.

- **Use it for:** Support, relationships, assistants.
- **Not for:** Private or invented customer chats.
- **Look:** A neutral original chat design, very large text.
- **Motion:** Messages arrive with human timing.
- **Type:** The messages are the film.
- **Sound:** Soft pops, a pause before the turn.
- **Build with:** HTML, fictional names.
- **Hook:** A message you should answer, but do not.
- **It fails when:** Copying a real app, long paragraphs.
