# Infinite zoom

Family: Cinematic 3D. Status: draft.

You keep falling into the next picture.

- **Use it for:** Scale, history, a platform with many layers.
- **Not for:** A message that needs holds to read.
- **Look:** Every picture hides the next one in its center.
- **Motion:** Constant ratio zoom, about 1.06 to 1.10 a frame in the fast parts, with calm stops.
- **Type:** One word per layer.
- **Sound:** A rising tone and one hit per layer.
- **Build with:** Nested stills or 3D scenes, matched scale at each join.
- **Hook:** Start inside something tiny and familiar.
- **It fails when:** The seam is visible, or there is no reason to arrive at the last picture.
