# Hands on a desk

Family: Live footage. Status: draft.

A quiet making process from above.

- **Use it for:** Physical products, sketching, food, unboxing.
- **Not for:** Pure software.
- **Look:** Locked overhead camera, one surface, careful objects.
- **Motion:** Hands enter, do, leave. Jump cuts in rhythm.
- **Type:** Tiny captions or none.
- **Sound:** Close real sound.
- **Build with:** Real footage.
- **Hook:** One satisfying action, already half done.
- **It fails when:** Messy framing, steps without a goal.
