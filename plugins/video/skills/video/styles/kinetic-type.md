# Kinetic type

Family: Minimal motion. Status: draft.

Only words, and they act.

- **Use it for:** A strong opinion, a manifesto, a voice clip.
- **Not for:** Visual products that must be seen.
- **Look:** One typeface, two colors, big scale jumps.
- **Motion:** Words do what they say: heavy drops, fast leaves. Hits on spoken syllables.
- **Type:** The whole film. Big, tight, maximum four words a frame.
- **Sound:** The voice leads, percussion follows word onsets.
- **Build with:** HTML or canvas with measured word times.
- **Hook:** The most surprising word fills the frame at frame 0.
- **It fails when:** Every word uses the same animation.
