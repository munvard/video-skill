# One joke

Family: Story and comedy. Status: draft.

A setup, a turn, and it ends.

- **Use it for:** Daily posts, relatable problems.
- **Not for:** A complex product.
- **Look:** Plain real place or one drawn scene.
- **Motion:** Straight, then one unexpected cut.
- **Type:** The setup line only.
- **Sound:** Silence around the turn.
- **Build with:** Phone footage or simple animation.
- **Hook:** A situation people recognise at once.
- **It fails when:** It explains the joke.
