# Shape morph

Family: Minimal motion. Status: studied.

Quiet, exact, and every object turns into the next.

- **Use it for:** Software, AI, a personal brand, a calm feature film.
- **Not for:** A loud sale or a joke.
- **Look:** Over 80 percent empty frame, one accent color per world, hairlines and soft grain.
- **Motion:** Slow start, very fast middle, then a creep of 0.2 to 1 percent a frame so holds never freeze.
- **Type:** Few words, tight sans, entering by letter. In 9:16 keep 14 px or more at 360 px wide.
- **Sound:** One dry close sound per event. Remove the music before the biggest shape.
- **Build with:** Canvas, SVG or WebGL at 60 fps.
- **Hook:** One small moving thing in a very empty frame, plus a line that opens a question.
- **It fails when:** A cut without a shared shape, tiny type, a pretty film with no product.
