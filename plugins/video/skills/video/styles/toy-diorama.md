# Miniature world

Family: Cinematic 3D. Status: draft.

A tiny, detailed, bright world you want to touch, built like a premium product shot.

- **Use it for:** Explaining a process, a team, a place or a game.
- **Not for:** Serious medical, legal or security topics.
- **Look:** A detailed miniature with real materials (painted metal, glass, wood, fabric), saturated color, one palette per world, real shadows, tilt-shift focus. Never a cheap toy or voxel kit.
- **Motion:** Small characters act. The crane camera tilts between sets and dives into props.
- **Type:** Chunky words that stand inside the set.
- **Sound:** Playful percussion, small wooden and plastic sounds on each action.
- **Build with:** Blender with CC0 models, EEVEE for length and Cycles for hero frames.
- **Hook:** A small world doing something absurdly big.
- **It fails when:** It looks like a toy kit (blocky, pixel or voxel characters, flat plastic), characters sit still, worlds feel empty, the palette turns brown.
