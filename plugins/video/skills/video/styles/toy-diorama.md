# Toy diorama

Family: Cinematic 3D. Status: studied.

A tiny bright world you want to touch.

- **Use it for:** Explaining a process, a team, a place or a game.
- **Not for:** Serious medical, legal or security topics.
- **Look:** Low poly or toy plastic, saturated color, one palette per world, real shadows, shallow focus.
- **Motion:** Small characters act. The crane camera tilts between sets and dives into props.
- **Type:** Chunky words that stand inside the set.
- **Sound:** Playful percussion, small wooden and plastic sounds on each action.
- **Build with:** Blender with CC0 models, EEVEE for length and Cycles for hero frames.
- **Hook:** A small world doing something absurdly big.
- **It fails when:** Characters sit still, worlds feel empty, the palette turns brown.
