# Movie trailer

Family: Story and comedy. Status: draft.

A tiny problem treated as an epic.

- **Use it for:** Product humor, launches.
- **Not for:** Copying a real film.
- **Look:** Wide frames, heavy light, title cards.
- **Motion:** Slow build, stop, fast montage.
- **Type:** Three title cards at most.
- **Sound:** Low hits, riser, a silent gap.
- **Build with:** Blender or footage, original music.
- **Hook:** A deadly serious line about a small thing.
- **It fails when:** It is only serious, with no joke or stakes.
