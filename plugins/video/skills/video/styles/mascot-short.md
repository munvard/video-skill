# Mascot short

Family: Story and comedy. Status: draft.

A character wants something.

- **Use it for:** A brand with an original character.
- **Not for:** Borrowed characters.
- **Look:** One simple set, strong silhouette, expressive eyes.
- **Motion:** Anticipation, action, reaction. Hold on the face.
- **Type:** Almost none.
- **Sound:** Voice noises and foley, not words.
- **Build with:** Blender or 2D rig.
- **Hook:** The mascot is already in trouble.
- **It fails when:** It only waves and points.
