# One take with a thread

Family: Cinematic 3D. Status: proven.

A single camera move through several worlds, led by one glowing object.

- **Use it for:** A launch film, or any product that connects places or devices.
- **Not for:** A 10 second post, or a product with one screen.
- **Look:** Deep dark space, rich light, floor reflections, haze. Each act is its own lit world.
- **Motion:** The camera follows the thread, pushes into a screen that fills the frame, and comes out in the next world. It settles on every readable object.
- **Type:** Few big words, one at a time, in a fixed safe area.
- **Sound:** One motif sound for the thread, a build to one peak, silence before it.
- **Build with:** Blender for worlds and objects, matched frames at every join.
- **Hook:** Open on the payoff already happening, then rewind.
- **It fails when:** Joins flash, windows swing without a reason, panels look like flat screenshots.
