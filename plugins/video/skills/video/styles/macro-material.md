# Macro material

Family: Cinematic 3D. Status: draft.

So close you can almost feel it.

- **Use it for:** Hardware, beauty, food, watches, fabric.
- **Not for:** Software with no physical object.
- **Look:** Extreme close-ups, one hard rim light, real dust and imperfections.
- **Motion:** Very slow slides and rack focus. One fast reveal of the whole object.
- **Type:** Almost none, small and late.
- **Sound:** Near silence, real contact sounds, one deep note at the reveal.
- **Build with:** Blender Cycles or real macro footage.
- **Hook:** A texture nobody can name yet.
- **It fails when:** Everything is equally sharp, or the reveal comes after the viewer left.
