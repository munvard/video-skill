# Paper collage

Family: Handmade and graphic. Status: draft.

Cut paper and photos on a table.

- **Use it for:** Culture, travel, food, magazines, history.
- **Not for:** Precise software steps.
- **Look:** Torn edges, real shadows, mixed scale, limited palette.
- **Motion:** Pieces slide and flip on twos with small settling.
- **Type:** Cut letters and tape.
- **Sound:** Paper, scissors, brushed drums.
- **Build with:** 2D layers in 3D space with shadows.
- **Hook:** A hand places one unexpected piece.
- **It fails when:** Flat pieces with no shadow.
