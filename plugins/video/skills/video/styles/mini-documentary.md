# Mini documentary

Family: Live footage. Status: draft.

A real person and what changed.

- **Use it for:** Customers, founders, craft, impact.
- **Not for:** No real person or permission.
- **Look:** Natural light, wide place shots, hands, face.
- **Motion:** Slow, observed, few moves.
- **Type:** Name of the place, few captions.
- **Sound:** The person's voice, sparse score.
- **Build with:** Real footage, written consent.
- **Hook:** A detail that raises a question.
- **It fails when:** Praise without a story.
