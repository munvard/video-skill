# POV moment

Family: Live footage. Status: draft.

You are the person it happens to.

- **Use it for:** Phones, wearables, daily tools.
- **Not for:** Abstract services.
- **Look:** Eye height, natural light, real hands and place.
- **Motion:** Handheld, cut on gaze.
- **Type:** One situation line.
- **Sound:** Place sound first, music late.
- **Build with:** Phone footage, real product.
- **Hook:** A moment everyone has had.
- **It fails when:** Acting that looks like an ad.
