# Made to travel

Read this when the user wants reach: "viral", "more views", "grow my page", "a hook". Nobody can promise it. What can be done is to raise the chance on every post and learn from each one.

## What is known
| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| TikTok ranks by a predicted chance that a person will finish, skip, like or share. Finishing a longer video is a strong signal. Follower count and old hits are not direct factors. | [How TikTok recommends](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you), [The For You feed](https://www.tiktok.com/privacy/blog/the-for-you-feed/en) |
| TikTok tells creators to catch attention early and keep it. Adding #foryou does not improve the chance. | [5 tips for creators](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/5-tips-for-tiktok-creators?lang=en) |
| YouTube shows "stayed to watch" for Shorts: the share of times people watched and did not swipe. An engaged view means staying past the first seconds. | [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217?hl=en&co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts), [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-monetization-qualified-watch-hours-shorts-views/) |
| Instagram ranks each surface differently, from a person's activity and facts about the post. Its head has named watch time, likes and sends as important, as reported by third parties. | [Instagram](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained), [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389) |
| In a study of shared news, emotions that wake people up (awe, anger, anxiety) spread more than quiet ones such as sadness. Surprising and useful content also spread more. | [Berger and Milkman, 2012](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/Virality.pdf) |

Platforms change. Check the current page before quoting one, and never state a made-up percentage or "the algorithm wants". Awe, surprise and use are safe ground. Do not build on anger, fear or shame.

## Think before making
1. **One person.** Name who sends it to whom, and the message they type with it ("this is so you", "look at this", "you need this"). If you cannot write that message, the idea is not ready.
2. **One reason to send.** Pick: it makes them look good, it helps a friend, it says something they feel, it is beautiful enough to show, it starts a friendly argument, it makes them laugh.
3. **One question** that opens before second 2 and closes near the end.
4. **The turn.** What does the viewer believe at second 3 that is wrong by second 10?
5. **Fit.** Would the hook attract people who could love this product, or only people passing by? Views from the wrong people are a cost.

## The first two seconds
Write ten hooks in at least five different forms. Draw frame 0 for each. Remove every one that needs sound, needs the caption, needs the brand to be known, or could open any other video.

| Form | What frame 0 does | Danger |
|---|---|---|
| Payoff first | the best moment is already happening | nothing left to wait for |
| Impossible picture | something that cannot be there | no link to the idea |
| Wrong order | the end, then "four minutes earlier" | the middle is dull |
| Open task | something is about to fail or finish | a slow result |
| Shared moment | the exact second everyone has lived | generic acting |
| Claim with a cost | a true statement people will test | a claim you cannot prove |
| Change of scale | tiny made huge, or huge made tiny | only a trick |
| Almost complete | one missing piece | the piece is obvious |
| Sound made visible | the picture moves to a sound you can imagine | dead without sound |
| A character in trouble | a clear want and a block | the viewer does not care |

Then pick three for a test. Change only the hook when you test hooks.

## Keep them
- Give something new before each place a viewer could leave: about every 2 to 3 seconds in fast styles, a clear promise in slow ones.
- Cut every beat that the viewer already knows will happen.
- The peak is the reason to send. Hold it long enough to enjoy.
- The end gives a loop, an answer or one action. Never all three.
- Post text adds one thing the film does not say. One clear question may invite comments, only if you want the answers. No bait, no fake scarcity, no invented proof.

## Score before building
Score the plan from 0 to 2 on each line. Build only at 14 or more, with no zero in the first four.

1. A stranger stops at frame 0 on a phone
2. Clear with the sound off
3. True, and shown instead of said
4. A person has a reason to send it
5. One idea, told in one sentence
6. A turn the viewer does not expect
7. Something here has not been seen this week
8. The right people would stop
9. Beauty or skill that can be felt
10. The end is worth reaching

Ask someone, or a fresh agent given only the frames, to say what it is about and where they would leave. Do not give them your explanation.

## After posting
Read numbers against the account's own normal, not against famous posts. Same time after posting, every time.

| What you see | Likely place to work |
|---|---|
| Many leave in the first seconds | frame 0, first line, wrong audience |
| A drop at one later moment | that beat: cut it or move the turn up |
| Watched to the end, few sends | no reason to send, or a flat peak |
| Sent a lot, few visits | the link between idea and product |
| Good rate, small reach | one post is a small sample: test again |

Keep one line per post in `.video/results.md`: date, idea, hook form, styles, length, numbers, one lesson. Every fifth post, try something far away. A style earns **proven** here, with numbers, never by taste alone.
