# Checklist

**Blocker:** false claim, private data, copied work, a broken frame or sound, a word needed for the story that cannot be read. **Major:** a stranger would scroll, misunderstand or feel something is cheap. Fix both. Note the rest.

1. Frame 0 alone on a phone: would a stranger stop?
2. Sound off: what does a stranger think it is about at 2 s, at the middle, at the end?
3. Sheet at 2 frames a second: any empty, dark, blank or repeated stretch with no reason?
4. Every join at 10 or more frames a second, then the exact two frames: ghost, jump, flash, double text?
5. Every object fully in frame when it is the subject? Anything cut by accident?
6. Crop interfaces and characters at 720 px: placeholder icons, square corners, missing hands, wrong eyes?
7. Each camera move: what does it follow? Does it go in and straight back out?
8. Text at 360 px wide, contrast, time on screen, safe area, spelling.
9. Last frame against frame 0 when it loops, sound too.
10. Loudness, true peak, voice over music and effects, effects on their events. Listen, or say that nobody has.
11. Every claim against a source. Every asset against a licence.
12. Names, mails, paths, notifications and keys in any frame: search with OCR and by eye.
13. Side by side with the last version people liked: is any scene flatter, emptier or more ordinary?
