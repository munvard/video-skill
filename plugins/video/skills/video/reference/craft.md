# Craft

## Story
- Frame 0 is full, bright enough and already about the idea. No logo opening, no fade from black.
- One idea. One question that keeps the viewer. One peak. A hold after the peak.
- The best ability gets time. A list of features gets none.
- End on a reason to act or on a loop. The loop is measured, not hoped for.

## Picture and motion
- The camera follows something: a hand, a cursor, ink, light, a character. Then it settles. Motion added to look busy is a fault.
- Do not swing flat panels, whip, flash the frame white or cut only for energy unless the chosen style is about that.
- Joins, best first: no join; a screen or shape that fills the frame; a matched object; the same place in the dark. Measure the two frames at a matched join: mean difference under 2 percent.
- Slow start, fast middle, long arrival. In a hold keep a tiny creep (0.2 to 1 percent a frame in calm styles) so it does not look frozen.
- Motion blur needs real sub-frames. Separate ghost copies are a fault.
- Give each world its own light. One subject owns the frame.
- Interfaces may be stylised, but the ability must be true and the shapes must look finished. Real recordings are for moments people would doubt.

## Type
- At 360 px wide every needed word is at least 14 px tall. In 9:16 keep words in x 60 to 940 and y 250 to 1450 of 1080x1920.
- One text idea at a time. At least 1 second each, longer for a sentence.
- A subtitle never covers the action or a face and does not repeat large words already on the screen.

## Sound
- One sound for one visible event. Leave silence before the most important one.
- Voices 8 dB over music or more. Natural reading, words placed by measured onset.
- Master about -14 LUFS, true peak under -1 dBTP. Use music with a licence you can show, and credit it.
