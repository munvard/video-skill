# Make or learn a style

## From a reference video
1. Extract every frame. List the shots: start, end, what moves, how it becomes the next shot.
2. Measure easing: track one object's position for the move and fit the curve. Note holds and the share of still frames.
3. Measure type height as a share of the frame, words on screen, empty space, colors, camera moves.
4. Measure sound: tempo, cuts against beats, each effect and the event it belongs to, loudness over time, silence.
5. Write 8 to 12 rules that would let someone make a different film with the same feeling.
6. Write what is weak in it. View counts show reach, not the reason for it. Say what cannot be known from the file.
7. Write a style card with no shot, line, layout, character or sound from the source. A person who knows the source must not call the new film a copy.

## From nothing
Choose one unusual limit (one line, one room, one color, one sound, one camera, twelve poses a second). Choose one thing that changes through the whole film. Make a 3 second proof. If it looks like a template, change the limit.

## The card
Title, family, status, one sentence of feeling, then: Use it for, Not for, Look, Motion, Type, Sound, Build with, Hook, It fails when. Under 150 words. Status starts at **draft**. **studied** needs frame measurements. **proven** needs a finished film and real viewer reactions. Save the user's own cards in `.video/styles/`, and read them together with the index.
